[h1]Advanced Inventory System[/h1]
An Arma 3-style inventory for Garry's Mod: what you wear is what you carry. Helmet, vest, backpack, uniform pockets and weapons all have their place on your character, and every item takes room according to its size, not a slot.

[b]Full feature list, settings, developer guide and issues: [url=https://github.com/milou2764/gmod-inventory]github.com/milou2764/gmod-inventory[/url][/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Capacity, not slots:[/b] uniform, vest and backpack each hold so many points; drag and drop, stacks, ammo boxes as your reserve.
[*][b]Gear on the body:[/b] helmets, vests, backpacks, gas masks, night vision and radios worn on the player, fitted to the 87 default playermodels and 94 popular Workshop ones.
[*][b]Weapons on the body:[/b] long guns slung, on the back or in front; armour by body part.
[*][b]Death and looting:[/b] bodies keep their gear; search them, or search other players (illegal items, confiscation).
[*][b]Storage:[/b] permanent, shared or personal crates, locks with codes, banks, loot tables, vehicle trunks.
[*][b]DarkRP ready:[/b] purchases go to the inventory, job and jail keep it, optional inventory per job.
[*][b]Any weapon base[/b] (attachments kept for ARC9, TFA, CW 2.0), grenades as items, usable items, crafting (off by default).
[*][b]Admin menu[/b] for inventories and every setting, CAMI privileges, logs. Server-authoritative and rate limited.
[*]Five languages, three themes. Lua only: every model comes with Garry's Mod.
[/list]

[h2]Compatible gear[/h2]
When a server has them, these addons' models become inventory items fitted to every playermodel (nothing of them is shipped): Fallout 76 and Fallout 3 backpacks, Star Wars helmets, TF2 Helmets of Victory, tank crew helmets, the Amnesia Adrian helmet, DayZ police vest, Petruz PMC vests, H.E.V tactical vest.

[h2]Getting started[/h2]
Subscribe (server and players), open the inventory with I or !inv, admin menu with advinv_admin.
