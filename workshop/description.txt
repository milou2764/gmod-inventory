[h1]Advanced Inventory System[/h1]
An Arma 3-style inventory for Garry's Mod: what you wear is what you carry. Helmet, vest, backpack, pockets and weapons all have their place on your character, and items take room by their size, not a slot.

[list]
[*][b]Capacity, not slots:[/b] uniform, vest and backpack hold so many points; drag and drop, ammo boxes as your reserve.
[*][b]Gear and weapons on the body,[/b] fitted to the 87 default playermodels and 114 Workshop ones.
[*][b]Looting and searching:[/b] bodies keep their gear; crates, banks, vehicle trunks.
[*][b]DarkRP ready, any weapon base[/b] (attachments kept for ARC9, ArcCW, TFA, CW 2.0, Sandstorm SWEPs), grenades as items.
[*][b]Admin menu,[/b] CAMI privileges, server-authoritative. Five languages, Lua only.
[/list]

[b]Supported addons[/b] (gear extensions, compatible gear, weapon bases): [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3845634999]the collection[/url].
[b]Features, settings, developer guide:[/b] [url=https://github.com/milou2764/gmod-inventory]GitHub[/url].

Open the inventory with I or !inv; admin menu: advinv_admin.
